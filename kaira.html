Where Art Meets Appetite

Crimson Cup is an independent art café in Ahmedabad where creativity brews alongside specialty coffee. We offer a thoughtfully curated menu of handcrafted beverages, all-day bites in an inspiring, art-filled space. What sets us apart is our unique blend of café culture and artistic expression — from rotating local art displays to cozy nooks perfect for reading, creating, or catching up with friends. Whether you’re here for a work session, a casual meet-up, or simply to soak in the ambience, Crimson Cup is a space where art and community come together over a great cup of coffee.
